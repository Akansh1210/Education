# 📚 Learning Is a Process

### An Open Educational Knowledge Repository

**Learn. Explore. Reflect. Improve. Repeat.**

Welcome to **Learning Is a Process**, an open educational knowledge repository created to explore, understand, document, and share knowledge about education and learning.

Education is not limited to classrooms, textbooks, or examinations. It is a continuous process of questioning, discovering, reflecting, and developing new perspectives.

This repository brings together educational theories, psychology, pedagogical approaches, curriculum frameworks, teaching resources, research materials, and reflections to support students, teachers, researchers, and teacher educators.

---

## 🎯 Our Vision

To create an accessible and organized educational knowledge resource that connects theoretical understanding with practical teaching and learning experiences.

## 🌱 Our Mission

- Make educational knowledge accessible to everyone.
- Simplify complex educational theories and concepts.
- Connect educational research with classroom practices.
- Promote reflective, inclusive, and learner-centred education.
- Encourage collaborative learning and knowledge sharing.
- Support continuous professional development of teachers.

## 🧠 Our Learning Philosophy

Learning is an active and continuous process.

We believe that:

1. Every learner has the potential to grow.
2. Learning happens through experience, reflection and interaction.
3. Knowledge becomes meaningful when connected to real life.
4. Mistakes are opportunities for learning.
5. Education should encourage curiosity, creativity and critical thinking.
6. Teachers are facilitators of learning, not merely transmitters of information.

1. Explore the folders according to your area of interest.

5. Suggest corrections, improvements and additional resources.

## 📚 Academic Integrity

This repository encourages responsible academic learning.

- Cite original authors and sources.
- Respect copyright and intellectual property.
- Do not upload pirated books or unauthorized PDFs.
- Distinguish evidence-based information from personal reflections.
- Verify educational policies and references using official sources.
